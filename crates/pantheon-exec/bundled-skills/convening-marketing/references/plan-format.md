# Marketing Plan Format

Deliver the round-table dialogue first, then this plan. Every factual claim carries a source link; every unknown carries `[ADD: ...]` or `[ESTIMATE]`.

## 1. The Round Table (dialogue)

- CMO-moderated; each agent speaks in its own named voice.
- Intelligence presents the market as found; Strategy defends its choices; Execution reports constraints; disagreements argued to a decision.
- Evidence anchored: when an agent cites research, link it.

## 2. The Marketing Plan

### Executive summary

Product, primary target, positioning, top 3 channels, and the single most important action for the next 30 days.

### Market and consumer snapshot

- Category dynamics (growth, trends, seasonality), linked
- The counted consumer base: segments with size, behavior evidence, JTBD, and where they gather, linked

### Competitive landscape

Teardown table: competitor, price, positioning, channels, strengths, exploitable weakness (linked). Saturation verdict and the attack surface.

### Positioning and messaging

- Primary segment (and the runner-up, with why it lost)
- Positioning statement, built slot-by-slot from evidence
- One-liner, message pillars, tagline candidates
- Objection playbook (objection in real customer words to response)

### Pricing direction

Recommendation, competitive anchors, willingness-to-pay evidence, and stated assumptions.

### Channel plan

Ranked channels with rationale, estimated costs `[ESTIMATE]`, and the first-year rough split.

### Copy pack

Editor-approved sample copy, each piece labeled with its message pillar:

- Tagline finalists (with rejected variants and one-line reasons)
- Ad variants per chosen channel
- Social post sequence (primary platform)
- Email sequence (launch + first nurture)
- Landing page hero + section copy
- Long-form seeds: headlines and angles with sourcing notes

### The first 90 days

Week-by-week milestones per channel, launch moments, and a sustainable weekly cadence for the actual team size.

### Measurement

North-star metric, funnel KPIs per channel, starting targets `[ESTIMATE]`, review cadence, kill criteria for tactics.

### Risks and open questions

Top risks to the plan, and every `[ADD: ...]` the user must fill in from internal data (current sales, customer lists, actual budget).

### Sources

Principal links, grouped by section.
