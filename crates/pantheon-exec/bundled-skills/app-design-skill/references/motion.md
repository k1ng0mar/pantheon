# Motion

Motion is the highest-leverage polish surface and the easiest to overdo.

- Frequency gate: 100+ times a day = platform default; tens a day = under 150ms; occasional = standard motion
- Name the purpose: feedback, spatial continuity, state change, explanation, delight
- Springs for gestures: start from live value, hand release velocity into the spring
- Everything else: under 300ms, strong ease-out
- Respect Reduce Motion: spatial motion collapses to cross-fades
