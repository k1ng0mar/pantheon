# A smoother, more reliable iPhone Duo

## Simulators

- **Folding no longer freezes PandaCode.** In 0.3.16 the loading spinner on the Duo's display redrew the whole screen image on every frame and could stall the window until the new screen arrived. It now costs almost nothing, and the 3D view stops drawing when nothing moves.
- **Folds happen first and always land.** The 3D Duo folds the moment you click; the simulator follows in the background. Clicking several poses quickly ends on the last one, and only that one is sent. Closing is reliable again: the hinge sweeps for 0.2 s instead of jumping, waits for the previous switch to settle, and recovers by way of a half-open position if the simulator misses a fold. Ten open/close cycles in a row each finished in about 1.2 s.
- **Agents tap the right thing on the inner display.** With the Duo open, finding a button by its label returned the cover display's position, so taps landed in the wrong place. PandaCode now reads element positions on the inner display from the simulator itself, and agents can find app content there that was missing before.

## Known limitations

- Describing the inner display takes 2–4 seconds longer than the cover display.
- If the simulator's SpringBoard gets stuck mid-fold, folds stop switching displays until the simulator restarts.
- iPhone Duo needs Xcode 27.1 or later on macOS.
