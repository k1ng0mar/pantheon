How it works

The AI is replaceable. Your assistant is not.

Pantheon sits between you and the AI model. You talk to Pantheon, it talks to the model, and it keeps track of everything. Change models any time. Your assistant stays the same.

A memory that does not forget.

Most AI chatbots forget everything when you close them. Pantheon writes every conversation down on your computer, so it builds on what it learned yesterday instead of starting over every morning.

You stay in control.

Pantheon can read files, run commands, and change things on your computer, but only with your say so. Anything that matters gets a clear yes or no from you first. It never does something important behind your back.

Use any model you like.

The AI model is just one part of the setup, and it is easy to swap. Try a new one, go back to the old one, use different models for different jobs. Your conversations, memory, and settings stay put.
