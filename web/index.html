Install Pantheon How it works

What it does

It remembers.

Every conversation is saved on your machine. Ask about something from last week and it still knows.

It asks first.

Before doing anything that matters, like deleting files or pushing code, it stops and asks you.

It does not lose your work.

Close your laptop in the middle of a task. When you come back, it picks up right where it left off.

Your data stays home.

Everything lives on your computer. There is no account, no cloud, nothing uploaded.

Two steps to a working assistant

Step 1

Install it.

One command. Works on Linux, macOS, and Windows.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/k1ng0mar/pantheon/master/install.sh | bash

Step 2

Tell it which AI to use.

Run pantheon setup and answer a few questions. Then just type pantheon and start talking.

pantheon setup

Questions

▸ Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can use a chat app, you can use Pantheon. You type, it answers, and it can use tools on your computer when you ask it to.

▸ Which AI do I talk to?

Whichever you like. Pantheon works with OpenAI, Anthropic, and others. You bring your own API key, and you can switch models any time without losing anything.

▸ Where does my data go?

Nowhere. It stays on your computer. There is no account to sign up for and nothing is uploaded.

▸ What does it cost?

Pantheon itself is free. You pay your AI provider for what you use, the same as you would talking to them directly.
